# Example Actor Mode script. Say "action mode one" to start it, "stop" to end it.
# NOUS: lines are hers (a line with no label is hers too). Any other CAPITAL label
# (YOU, MOM, KID...) is a person's line: she waits until you say it, or "next line".
# Cues in [brackets]: face, lights, dance, head, music, pause.
[lights: blue]
[face: happy]
NOUS: Hello there! Welcome to my very first show.
YOU: What can you do?
NOUS: I can make faces.
[face: thinking]
NOUS: I can look around.
[head: look around]
NOUS: And I can dance!
[dance: wiggle]
[pause: 3]
NOUS[es]: Y hablo un poco de español.
[lights: rainbow]
NOUS: Thank you for watching!
